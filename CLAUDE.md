# riz-ai-business-website

Read `README.md` first — it covers the routes, the two coexisting design
systems, the type and colour rules, and the known issues.

`DESIGN-NOTES.md` records why the homepage looks the way it does: what was
taken from buildwithnav.com, what was deliberately left out, and the open
decisions. Read it before redesigning the homepage.

This is a standard Next.js App Router project. Nothing exotic — no custom
compiler, no patched framework.
